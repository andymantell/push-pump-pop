PUMP

Tap on the way down a slope, a roller or a berm to go faster. Downhill does most of the work, but good pumping wins races. Going too far in the air? Tap on the way down to drop onto the landing.

TRICK swipe on the right half

↑ Bunny Hop   ← Whip   → Tabletop   ↓ Turndown

Throw the bike sideways, lay it flat, or twist the bars down. Swipe again once it's back for another. Land straight or you bail!

ROCK GARDENS on the rocky bits

The rocks knock your weight forwards and backwards: watch the balance bar and tilt to keep it in the middle. Too far forward and you go over the bars!

SKINNIES planks and rails

Hop on and ride along the top. Manual or Stoppie along a skinny for lots more points. Swipe up to hop off.

Keyboard: Space pump, arrows trick, hold I J K L to hold, Z X spin, hold Q E to tilt

PADDLE tap

Every tap is a paddle stroke. Tap on the way down each wave to go faster. Tap just as you reach a marked rock to Splat it: the boat stands up on its tail against the rock.

BOOF tap on the lip

A paddle stroke right on the lip of a weir or waterfall boofs you off it into the air. On flat water there's nothing to boof off, so a stroke just paddles.

TILT tip the iPad like a steering wheel

Tip it to lift the bow or the stern. The boat follows how far you tip: a little tip, a little lift. On flat water that's a Bow Stall or Stern Stall. Level the iPad before you land!

SPIN drag your thumb sideways

Drag left or right and the boat spins round you, like your body is the axle from your head to your bottom. A quarter of the way across the screen is a whole turn. Tilt the bow up first and the ends swing up and over as it spins.

MOVES do it and the game spots it

There are no buttons for tricks. Move the boat like a real playboater and the game names what you did:

In a hole: Flatspin (spin it flat), Blunt (tilt up, spin half round), Cartwheel (tilt up, spin round and round).

In the air: Air Blunt (half way round), Pan-Am (half way round with the bow tipped up), Helix (all the way round), McNasty (one and a half times round).

Land pointing up or down the river, not stood on an end, or you capsize!

CAPSIZE upside down!

Tap tap tap fast to Eskimo roll back up. Too slow and you swim, and lose a life.

Some holes are sticky: go in slowly and they hold you. Tap to paddle out, or go fast and punch straight through.

STANDING WAVE the bonus level

Surf a real river wave in a little playboat, facing upstream. Tap to rock up and down the face. Paddle as you climb up to the crest to boof into the air, then throw as many moves as you can before time runs out.

Keyboard: Space paddle, hold Q E to tilt, Z X to spin half round
