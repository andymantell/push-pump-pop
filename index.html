TRICK GUIDE

PUMP

Tap anywhere on the way down a slope to build speed. Going too far in the air? Tap on the way down to drop faster onto the landing.

FLIP swipe on the right half

↑ Ollie   ← Kickflip   → Heelflip   ↓ Pop Shove-it

↙ 360 Flip (tre flip)   ↘ Hardflip. Swipe down and to the side on a slant. They take longer, so save them for big air!

Land before the board stops spinning or you bail!

GRAB swipe and hold on the left half

↑ Indy   ↓ Melon   ← Nosegrab   → Tailgrab

The longer you hold, the more it scores. Let go before you land!

SPIN swipe sideways along the bottom

→ Frontside   ← Backside. Swipe further for a 360.

Keep spinning the same way and it adds up: 180, 360, 540, 720! A backflip with a 180 is a Flair, with a 360 a Cash Roll. A frontflip with a backside 540 is a Misty. A 360 Flip with a backside 180 is a Bigspin Flip.

TILT tip the iPad like a steering wheel

On the ground: right side up for a Manual, down for a Nose Manual. Manuals keep your combo going.

In the air: the same tilt does a Backflip or Frontflip.

GRIND land on a rail, then swipe on the right

← Boardslide   → Nosegrind   ↓ 5-0   ↑ ollie off. Fancier grinds score more.

MOUNTAIN BOARD on the farm

Big tyres for the mud, and your feet are strapped in like on a snowboard, so the right-side swipes are the same: ← BS Shifty   → FS Shifty   ↓ Tuck Knee.

Keyboard: Space pump, arrows flip, C V for a 360 Flip or Hardflip, hold I J K L to grab, Z X spin, hold Q E to tilt

Controller: A pump, flick the right stick to flip (down-left or down-right for a 360 Flip or Hardflip), hold LT or RT to grab, left stick sideways to spin, back or forwards to flip. In menus: d-pad to move, A to pick, B to go back, Menu to pause

ROCK GARDENS on the rocky bits

The rocks knock your weight forwards and backwards: watch the balance bar and tilt to keep it in the middle. Too far forward and you go over the bars!

SKINNIES planks and rails

Hop on and ride along the top. Manual or Stoppie along a skinny for lots more points. Swipe up to hop off.

Laid out like Skate 3.

A or X PUMP

Press on the way down a slope to build speed, just like tapping the screen.

RIGHT STICK flick for tricks

Flick the right stick for a trick, the same way you'd swipe on the right of the screen: ↑ Ollie, ← Kickflip, → Heelflip, ↓ Shove-it on the skateboard. Flick down on a slant (↙ ↘) for a 360 Flip or Hardflip. On a rail it changes your grind. Let the stick come back to the middle before the next one.

Push it gently down and hold for a Manual, or gently up for a Nose Manual.

LT RT GRAB

Hold a trigger in the air to grab the board. Pull the trigger and straight away point the right stick to pick the grab: ↑ Indy, ↓ Melon, ← Nosegrab, → Tailgrab. With the stick in the middle, RT is an Indy and LT a Melon. Let go of the trigger before you land!

LEFT STICK spins and flips

In the air, push it sideways to spin. A flick is a 180; hold it for a 360, and keep holding for more. LB and RB spin too. Pull it back for a Backflip or push it forwards for a Frontflip. Push it on a slant to flip and spin at once: diagonally back and to the side, held for one flip, is a backflip 360, a Cash Roll! On the bike, back and forwards is how you balance through rock gardens.

MENUS

D-pad or left stick to move, A to pick, B to go back. Menu pauses while you ride, and View watches your replay.

Works with an Xbox controller, or any controller your iPad or computer knows. Each ride's own trick names are on its tab.
