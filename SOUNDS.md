# Sound credits

Every sound in Push Pump Pop is **synthesised in the browser** with the Web Audio API, in
`src/audio.ts`. The game ships no recorded sound files, samples or music, so there are no
outside sounds to credit and no third-party licences apply. The synthesis code was written
for this game and lives in this repository.

| Sound | How it's made |
| --- | --- |
| Rolling on concrete (Sunny Park) | Brown noise roar plus a white noise band, with a two-wheel "tk-tk" over each slab joint every 3 m |
| Rolling on boardwalk planks (Beach) | A hollow, resonant low band, and a knock over every gap between boards |
| Rolling on tar and gravel (Rooftops) | A duller roar with lots of little gravel crackles |
| Carving snow (Snowy Mountain) | A bright filtered hiss and crunch, no wheel roar (it's a snowboard) |
| Rolling on sand (Desert) | A gritty, hissy band and dense crackles |
| Rolling on temple flagstones (Jungle) | A stone roar with uneven, clunky joints |
| Rolling on lava rock (Volcano) | A heavy, crunchy roar |
| Landings and bails | A low pitched thump, and a noise burst shaped to the surface |
| Grinds | White noise rung through tuned resonances: metal rails ring, box ledges scrape, jungle logs rasp. Boardslides sound lower than 50-50s |
| Ollie pop, flips, spins, grabs, pumps, pushing | Short filtered noise bursts and pitched sweeps |
| Gaps | A splash with bubbles (water), a sizzle (lava), or a cartoon whistle down |
| Ambience | Filtered noise beds (breeze, waves, city traffic, mountain wind, volcano rumble, rain, thunder) and synthesised calls (birds, gulls, crickets, frogs, a hawk, car horns, lava pops, hens, cows, a rooster and an owl on the farm). Mud squelches under the wheels. Candy Kingdom has music-box twinkles, chocolate bubbles, a crunchy biscuit path, glassy candy-cane grinds and a boing on jelly pads |
| Menus and scoring | Short sine and triangle wave chimes |

## Adding outside sounds

If a recorded sound is ever added, it must be free to use (CC0 or CC-BY only), and gets a row
here with: the file in the repo, what it's used for, the author, a link to the source, and
the licence (with a link to it). CC-BY sounds must also be credited in the game itself.
